Make a 36-second piece on a 1824×1200 canvas that dives through screens.
Four scenes of eight, eight, eight and twelve seconds. In each of the
first three, a device from the shared library — a laptop, then a tablet,
then a phone — stands on a floor under a slowly scrolling patterned
background with a soft shadow beneath it; nothing else moves. Its screen
shows a still of the NEXT scene's first frame. After a three-second hold
the camera straightens and the device grows until the picture on its
screen fills the canvas exactly, and the piece cuts to that next scene —
so the cut is invisible and the next device is already there. The fourth
scene is the cube from the cube demo turning under two bursts of
confetti, with "Made with PromoShot" arriving at the end.
